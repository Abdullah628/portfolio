overview: 'Zenops Studio is a community-powered marketplace for digital assets — icons, illustrations, UI kits, mockups and more — where designers upload their work and earn from every sale. Buyers browse and search the catalog, pay by card, PayPal, or mobile wallet, and get instant access to their files. The harder problems were on the money side: payments run through multiple providers so customers in different regions can all check out, purchases have to stay correct even when a payment webhook fires twice, and every sale splits into contributor earnings that mature on a hold window before a payout can be requested. Downloads are locked to verified buyers through short-lived signed links that stop working if an order is refunded.'
